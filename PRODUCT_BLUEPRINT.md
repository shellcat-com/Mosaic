# Mosaic v3 product blueprint

Mosaic is a free iPhone app for schools, neighborhoods, workplaces, nonprofits, and friend groups to complete kindness activities together without points or rankings. A signed-in organizer creates one invitation-only Mosaic, writes the activities, selects reviewed public-domain artwork, chooses Sunwashed, Garden, or Afterglow, assigns 12, 24, or 36 shots per member, chooses a square tile goal from 9 through 100, and fixes the start and reveal time.

Every participant uses Sign in with Apple and a display name. Each account may confirm each activity once. The confirmation is self-attested, may contain a short note, and immediately claims one equal-size tile. There is no evidence, proof, approval, privacy mode, anonymous identity, organizer moderation, score, ranking, workspace, or paid tier.

The camera is a parallel photo experience. It captures still images only, screens them on-device, permanently develops the event film look, and uploads only the resulting JPEG. A kept photo consumes a shot; a retake does not. Photographers see their own sealed roll before reveal. Photos never create tiles and never enter activity records.

At reveal, all input closes. Contributed tiles turn first, then unfilled porcelain positions complete the selected artwork. Joined members receive three separate outcomes: Artwork, Kindness, and Photos. Kindness tiles expose the activity, optional note, contributor, and time. Photo details expose photographer and capture time in the gallery only.

Any member can create a personal recap from 1–24 gallery photos, ordered explicitly. Existing music and template personality remain, but the video contains only the developed photos. It has no artwork, notes, identities, activities, captions, title cards, statistics, or impact receipt, and it is rendered and shared locally.

The interface follows Mosaic's Living Kiln design system: tactile porcelain surfaces, warm editorial typography, direct object-first layouts, 44-point targets, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver, non-color state cues, and reduced-motion alternatives. Bubbbly/Ann Nguyen and the supplied Filmera/Tailii flows are credited as interaction inspiration only; Mosaic does not copy their code, assets, branding, or layouts.
