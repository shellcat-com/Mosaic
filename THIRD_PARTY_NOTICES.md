# Third-party notices

## Product and interaction references

Mosaic's Living Kiln interface is original work. The public portfolio of [Bubbbly by Ann Nguyen](https://www.bubbbly.com/) was used only as inspiration for warmth, tactile object-first presentation, and retro-camera personality. The Filmera/Tailii flows supplied by the project owner were used only to understand the mechanics of invitations, a sealed disposable roll, a shared post-event gallery, photo ordering, and personal recap export. Mosaic contains no copied source code, assets, branding, interface copy, or screen layouts from either reference.

## Fraunces

Mosaic bundles the Fraunces typeface under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The complete license is included at `Mosaic/Resources/Fonts/Fraunces-OFL.txt`.

## Museum artwork catalog

Mosaic's organizer catalog uses reviewed public-domain works made available by the Art Institute of Chicago under its CC0 Public Domain Designation. Each catalog record preserves the work's title, artist, date, museum URL, license label, accessibility description, and curated crop. The complete source list is maintained in `supabase/catalog/artic-museum-artworks.v1.json`.

The twelve catalog revision 2 additions and their downloaded review images are documented in `design/artwork-review/README.md`. Full catalog images are delivered on demand and are not bundled into the iOS app.
