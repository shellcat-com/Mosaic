## Description

Make kindness visible—without scores, rankings, or proof.

Mosaic lets a school, neighborhood, workplace, nonprofit, or friend group create one invitation-only kindness experience. An organizer chooses simple activities and a reviewed public-domain artwork, then shares a QR code or link. Every completed activity places one equal-size ceramic tile. At the fixed reveal time, those tiles turn over and the complete artwork appears.

Photos preserve the day, but never prove kindness. Mosaic's built-in still camera gives every member a limited disposable roll with one event-wide film look. Before reveal, each photographer sees only their own sealed roll. After reveal, joined members can browse the eligible gallery together.

Each member can make a personal vertical photo recap: choose 1–24 gallery photos, arrange them, pick a visual template and licensed music, preview, then save or share. Recaps contain photos only—never artwork, notes, activities, names, captions, rankings, or statistics.

Mosaic includes Sign in with Apple, fixed invitation-only membership, on-device sensitive-content screening, photo reporting, contributor blocking, photographer deletion, creator deletion, and in-app account deletion.

Organizer Plus is available as monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation. Subscriptions automatically renew unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and the account is charged for renewal within 24 hours before that period ends. You can manage or cancel in App Store account settings. A one-time Event Pass can be used to create one premium Mosaic without Plus.
