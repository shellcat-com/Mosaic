Privacy Policy

Effective August 20, 2026

Mosaic is designed around private evidence, separate consent, and equal participation. This policy describes the Reverie Hacks judge build and the application code in the public repository.

HomeTerms of Use

Information Mosaic handles

  • Account and profile: an anonymous Supabase user identifier, optional display name, privacy choices, and - outside the hackathon build - an Apple-linked account identifier.
  • User content: reflections, photos, short videos, receipts, shared moments, display-name choices, and consent settings supplied for a challenge.
  • Activity: challenge membership, contribution state, moderation decisions, tile placement, reveal state, and feature interactions needed to operate the experience.
  • Device services: notification or Live Activity tokens when those optional features are configured, plus local preferences, cached event data, file timestamps, and available storage checks.
  • Purchases: RevenueCat customer and entitlement status when billing is configured. The hackathon judge build has billing disabled.
  • Security data: Supabase may process connection metadata such as IP address and authentication logs to secure the service.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate participants, operate private challenges, authorize organizer review, create equal-weight tiles, synchronize reveals, generate consent-aware recaps, recover cached state, prevent abuse, and diagnose reliability or security problems. Mosaic does not sell personal information, serve advertising, or track people across apps or websites.

Visibility and sharing

Evidence is available only to its contributor and authorized organizers. Evidence approval is separate from permission to include a memory in the community reveal. Identity display and recap/export permission are separate choices. Realtime messages contain only sanitized invalidation identifiers; clients refetch data through row-level security. Infrastructure providers such as Supabase and, when enabled, RevenueCat process only the information needed to provide their services.

Storage, retention, and deletion

Local preferences and cached data remain on the device until they are cleared or the app is removed. Hosted challenge records and media remain until the related content, workspace, or account is deleted, or until the disposable hackathon project is retired after judging. Short-lived signed URLs protect private media access. Account deletion is available inside the app; a sole workspace owner must first transfer or delete the workspace. Apple subscriptions, if enabled later, must be cancelled separately through Apple.

Children, security, and choices

The judge build is intended for participants age 13 or older and uses synthetic demonstration data. Do not upload sensitive or real-world evidence to the public demo. Mosaic uses authenticated Edge Functions, explicit database grants, row-level security, private Storage buckets, and least-privilege access controls. No system can guarantee absolute security.

You may choose an anonymous display, decline community-memory inclusion, decline attribution, decline export permission, delete eligible content, or delete your account.

Contact and updates

Questions or deletion requests may be sent to biswas06@iastate.edu. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date on this page.
