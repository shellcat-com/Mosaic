Privacy Policy

Mosaic is built around private evidence, separate consent, and equal participation. This policy explains the information the app handles and the choices you control.

Effective August 21, 2026

On this page Scope Information we handle How we use information Sharing and visibility Retention and deletion Your choices Security Children International processing Contact

Mosaic does not sell personal information, serve behavioral advertising, or track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the Mosaic iOS application, its invitation links, this website, and the hosted services used to operate private kindness challenges. In this policy, “Mosaic,” “we,” and “us” refer to the independent developer of the Mosaic app.

A challenge organizer decides the purpose, membership, missions, and moderation rules for their private challenge. Mosaic supplies the product and privacy controls described here.

Device permissions

Mosaic asks for camera, microphone, photo library, notification, or photo-saving access only when you use the related feature. You can change these permissions in iOS Settings. Mosaic does not request location access, access your contacts, or use advertising identifiers.

3. How we use information

We use information only as reasonably necessary to:

  • create and authenticate guest or permanent accounts;
  • operate invitation-only challenges and enforce participant roles;
  • receive, privately review, and moderate contribution evidence;
  • create equal-weight tiles, synchronize reveals, and generate Impact Receipts;
  • create recaps and exports using only eligible, consented content;
  • deliver notifications or Live Activities you enable;
  • provide purchase access, restore entitlements, and prevent duplicate redemption;
  • respond to support, deletion, safety, or security requests; and
  • detect abuse, maintain reliability, and comply with applicable law.

4. Sharing and visibility

Inside a challenge

Private evidence is available only to the contributor and authorized organizers who need it for review. Evidence approval does not grant permission to publish a memory. Identity display, reveal inclusion, and recap/export permission are separate controls. Other participants see only the information permitted by the challenge rules and your choices.

Service providers

We use providers that process information on our behalf to operate the app:

  • Supabase provides authentication, database, private media storage, realtime updates, and server functions.
  • RevenueCat provides purchase entitlement and subscription-state management when paid features are enabled.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, push notification delivery, App Store purchases, subscription management, and platform services.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this public website and may process ordinary web request logs.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. We do not authorize them to use Mosaic data for behavioral advertising.

Legal and safety disclosures

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, respond to lawful process, protect the rights or safety of users or others, investigate abuse, or defend the service. If the service is reorganized or transferred, information may transfer with it subject to this policy and applicable law.

5. Retention and deletion

We keep information only while it is needed to provide Mosaic, support the challenge lifecycle, protect the service, resolve disputes, or satisfy legal obligations.

  • Account and challenge records generally remain while the account, challenge, or workspace is active.
  • Evidence and memories remain until the related content, account, challenge, or workspace is deleted, subject to organizer permissions and legal requirements.
  • Local data remains on your device until cleared by the app, account actions, or removal of the app.
  • Notification tokens are retained only while needed to provide enabled notifications and are removed or invalidated when no longer necessary.
  • Purchase and transaction records may be retained where required for accounting, fraud prevention, or legal compliance. Apple separately retains App Store transaction information.
  • Backups and security logs may remain for a limited period until overwritten or no longer needed for security and recovery.

Deleting an account removes the account and associated user-generated content from active systems unless retention is legally required. If you are the sole owner of a workspace, Mosaic asks you to transfer ownership or delete the workspace first so other members’ shared challenge data is not removed without warning.

6. Your choices

You can choose an anonymous or quiet identity, decline memory inclusion, decline attribution, decline recap/export permission, change device permissions, manage notification preferences, delete eligible content, or initiate account deletion.

To delete an account, open Mosaic → You → Account & privacy → Delete account. See the account deletion and privacy choices guide for the full process. Deleting a Mosaic account does not cancel an Apple subscription; subscriptions are managed through Apple.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or receive a copy of personal information, or object to certain processing. Contact us to make a request. We may need to verify your identity before completing it.

7. Security

Mosaic uses authenticated server functions, row-level database security, private media buckets, short-lived signed media links, explicit access roles, input validation, and consent-aware exports. Realtime messages contain limited invalidation identifiers; clients re-fetch protected data through access controls. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Mosaic is not directed to children under 13 and is not currently offered as a Made for Kids app. People under the minimum age required in their country should not use Mosaic without any consent required by law. Organizers using Mosaic with a school or youth group are responsible for appropriate authorization, supervision, and content rules. If you believe a child submitted personal information contrary to this policy, contact us so we can investigate and remove it where appropriate.

9. International processing

Mosaic and its providers may process information in countries other than your own. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international transfers. Local privacy rights may vary by location.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Mosaic changes. Material updates will be posted here with a revised effective date, and we will provide additional notice when required.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, email biswas06@iastate.edu. Please do not send private evidence, identity documents, passwords, or payment information by email unless we specifically request a safe verification step.
