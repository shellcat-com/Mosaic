Kindness with boundaries

Community Guidelines

Mosaic works when care is voluntary, evidence stays private, and every person is treated with dignity.

Effective August 21, 2026

On this pageCore principlesContent rulesSafety & privacyFor organizersReportsEmergencies

If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate trusted person. Mosaic is not monitored as an emergency channel.

1. Core principles

Kindness is voluntary

No one should be pressured to participate, disclose personal experiences, spend money, or complete an unsafe mission.

Every act has equal weight

Do not use Mosaic to rank people, shame non-participants, or turn care into status or competition.

Proof is private

Evidence exists for appropriate review—not public attention. Never repost another person’s evidence without separate permission.

Consent is specific

Permission for evidence, identity, a memory, and an export are different choices. Respect each one.

2. Content rules

Do not submit or create missions involving:

  • harassment, threats, hate, humiliation, bullying, or unwanted contact;
  • sexual exploitation or sexual content involving minors;
  • violence, self-harm encouragement, dangerous stunts, weapons, or illegal activity;
  • deception, fake evidence, impersonation, scams, or manipulated impact claims;
  • copyrighted or confidential material you do not have permission to use;
  • political coercion, discriminatory participation rules, or exploitative fundraising; or
  • spam, advertising, unrelated promotions, or attempts to move participants into unsafe contact.

3. Safety and privacy

Avoid faces, addresses, license plates, student identifiers, health information, payment details, confidential documents, and precise location clues in photos, videos, receipts, reflections, or shared memories. Use crop and redaction controls when a receipt or image includes information the organizer does not need.

Do not photograph, record, identify, or share another person without the permission required in your location. Never pressure someone to make private evidence public.

4. Extra responsibilities for organizers

Organizers should choose age-appropriate, accessible missions; describe acceptable evidence before participation; invite only intended members; review reports promptly; and avoid collecting more information than necessary. Schools, workplaces, and organizations must follow their own safeguarding, consent, records, and accessibility obligations.

Organizers must distinguish verified, confirmed, and self-attested activity. They may remove content or participants to protect the group, but should not disclose private evidence or the reason for a moderation decision to uninvolved participants.

5. Reporting and enforcement

Use available in-app report or organizer controls when possible. You can also email biswas06@iastate.edu. Include the type of concern, challenge name if safe, and enough context to investigate. Do not forward harmful media unless requested through a safe channel.

Depending on severity and context, a response may include hiding or removing content, revoking an invitation, restricting features, suspending an account, preserving limited evidence required for safety, or reporting unlawful conduct to the appropriate authority.

6. Emergencies and wellbeing

Mosaic is not a crisis service and cannot verify that a report will be read immediately. If you or another person is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For a school, youth, workplace, or community safety concern, also follow the reporting process of the responsible institution or trusted authority.
