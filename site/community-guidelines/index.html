Kindness with boundaries

Community Guidelines

Participation is voluntary. Every contribution has equal weight, and photos must respect the people in them.

Effective August 24, 2026

Mosaic is not an emergency channel. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate trusted person.

Be kind and safe

Do not create activities, notes, photos, or invitations involving harassment, hate, threats, humiliation, sexual exploitation, violence, self-harm encouragement, dangerous acts, illegal activity, scams, impersonation, spam, coercion, or discrimination. Do not pressure anyone to participate, spend money, disclose personal experiences, or appear in a photo.

Respect privacy and ownership

Capture people only with permission required in your location. Avoid addresses, license plates, student or health identifiers, financial details, confidential documents, and precise location clues. Upload only photos you have the right to share. Kindness confirmations are self-attested; do not use Mosaic to make false impact claims.

Reports, blocks, and removal

Use Report photo from photo detail for shared content that violates these rules. The photo is immediately quarantined from galleries and recap choices while the developer reviews it. Use Block to hide a contributor's photos from your own experience. Photographers can delete their photos and creators can delete their Mosaic. Serious or repeated violations may lead to content removal or account restriction.

Organizers

Choose lawful, accessible, age-appropriate activities and invite only intended members. Organizers do not receive a private approval queue and cannot inspect other members' photos before reveal. Schools, employers, and organizations must follow their own consent, safeguarding, records, and accessibility duties.
