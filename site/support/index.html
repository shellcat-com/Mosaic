We're here to help

Mosaic Support

Get help with accounts, invitations, camera access, photos, reports, recaps, or deletion.

Contact

Email biswas06@iastate.edu. Include your iOS version, app version, and a short description. Never email an Apple token, password, private key, or harmful photo.

Common help

  • Cannot join: Sign in with Apple, create a display name, and verify the reveal has not passed.
  • Camera unavailable: enable Camera for Mosaic in iOS Settings. Mosaic never needs microphone or Photo Library read access.
  • No shared photos yet: other members' photos stay sealed until reveal.
  • Shot restored: deleting your photo restores a shot only while the event remains active.
  • Report or block: open a shared photo after reveal and choose Report or Block.
  • Recap: choose 1–24 gallery photos; exports render locally and are not stored by Mosaic.

Response expectations

Safety and account-deletion requests receive priority. Mosaic is not continuously monitored and is not an emergency service.
