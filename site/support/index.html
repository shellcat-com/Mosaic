Common questions

Quick answers.

My invitation code is not working.

Check that the code was copied without spaces and that you are using the most recent invitation from the organizer. Codes may expire or be revoked. Ask the organizer for a new invitation if the challenge is still active.

Why can’t I see another person’s evidence?

That is intentional. Evidence is private to the contributor and authorized organizers. A community memory is a separate, explicitly approved item shown only after the challenge rules and consent choices allow it.

How do I change my name or privacy mode?

Open the challenge’s privacy or profile controls in Mosaic. Depending on the challenge state, choose first name, anonymous, or quiet participation. Some revealed or exported content may no longer be editable after it has been shared outside Mosaic.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open Mosaic’s billing screen and choose Restore Purchases. Make sure the device uses the Apple ID that made the purchase. If access still does not appear, email support with the product name and approximate purchase date—never send full payment details.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or use Apple’s subscription management page. Deleting Mosaic does not cancel an Apple subscription.

How do I request a refund?

App Store transactions and refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with the Apple ID used for the purchase.

How do I delete my account?

In Mosaic, open You → Account & privacy → Delete account. Sole workspace owners must transfer ownership or delete their workspace first. See the full deletion guide.

What should I include in a support email?

Tell us what you expected, what happened, your iPhone and iOS version, and whether the issue affects a guest or signed-in account. Include a screenshot only if it contains no private evidence, challenge codes, payment details, or personal information.

Still need a hand?

Email us with a short description of the issue. We will use your message only to respond, troubleshoot, and protect the service.
