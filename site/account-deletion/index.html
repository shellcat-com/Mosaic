Account deletion

You can initiate permanent deletion inside the app without contacting support.

Updated August 26, 2026

Delete in the app

  1. If you have Mosaic Plus, open the Mosaic Plus screen and choose Manage Subscriptions to cancel it if desired. Deleting your account does not cancel billing through Apple.
  2. Open You.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Review the permanent-deletion warning and confirm.

This deletes your account, memberships, contributions, photos, reports, blocks, and Mosaics you created from active systems. Limited backup, security, or legally required records may expire later under the Privacy Policy.
