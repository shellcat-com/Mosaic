Account deletion & privacy choices

Delete your Mosaic account from inside the app, manage eligible sharing choices, or ask us for help with a privacy request.

Last updated August 21, 2026

Account deletion is available directly in Mosaic. You do not need to email us or create a support ticket to start it.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Mosaic and select the You tab.
  2. Open Account & privacy.
  3. Select Delete account and review what will be removed.
  4. Confirm the deletion. Mosaic signs out the deleted account and returns the app to a fresh guest experience.

If you use Sign in with Apple, Mosaic deletes the linked Mosaic account. Platform-level Sign in with Apple authorization may also need to be revoked as part of the production deletion process.

What deletion removes

Deletion removes your account record and associated personal data from Mosaic’s active systems, including profile details, challenge memberships, user-generated evidence and memories, consent choices, and notification registrations, unless retention is legally required.

Aggregate counts or artwork state may be recalculated, removed, or preserved only in a form that no longer identifies you or exposes your submitted content. Limited information may remain temporarily in security logs or backups until those systems are overwritten or the information is no longer needed.

Before you delete

Sole workspace owners

Transfer ownership to another member or delete the workspace first. This prevents deletion from unexpectedly affecting other members’ shared challenges.

Apple subscriptions

Deleting your Mosaic account does not cancel an App Store subscription. Manage it in iOS Settings or on Apple’s subscriptions page.

Shared exports

A recap, poster, or image already saved or shared outside Mosaic cannot be recalled from someone else’s device or another service.

Deletion is permanent

Once completed, the account and eligible content cannot be restored. You may later use Mosaic again with a new guest or Apple-linked account.

Other privacy choices

You do not have to delete your account to change every privacy setting. Depending on the challenge state and your role, Mosaic lets you:

  • participate with a first name, anonymously, or quietly;
  • decline or withdraw eligible community-memory inclusion;
  • choose whether your name is attributed;
  • decline recap or export permission;
  • delete eligible evidence, memories, or challenge content;
  • turn off notification categories or device permissions; and
  • request access, correction, or a copy of personal information where applicable.

Need help with a privacy request?

Email biswas06@iastate.edu. Tell us the type of request and whether you used a guest or Sign in with Apple account. Do not email passwords, full receipts, identity documents, private evidence, or challenge codes. We may ask for a safe verification step before acting on account data.
